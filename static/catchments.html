Ten catchments, one highland basin

The Kotmale Reservoir draws from catchments across the central highlands tea country surrounding Nuwara Eliya. Eleven stations were originally sourced; one — Labukele — was excluded after being found identical to Hope Estate across all 25 years of record (9.7 km apart, both inside the same ~25 km reanalysis grid cell). A resolution finding specific to this dataset

Rainfall before 2017 came from a coarser ~25 km reanalysis grid than the ~9 km grid used from 2017 onward. Catchments within the same grid cell returned identical daily values pre-2017 — three underlying signals wearing ten name tags — before separating into fully independent readings from 2017.

Watawala and Oonoogaloya, the wettest catchments, sit on windward-facing slopes typical of the southwestern highland escarpment; Ambewela, the driest, lies in the higher, more sheltered plateau near Sri Lanka's coldest recorded weather station.
